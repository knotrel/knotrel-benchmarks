Status: source selection and adapter contracts. The [Abilene adapter integration
run](../../results/2026-10-03-topology-import-smoke/README.md) now pins and validates
one GraphML artifact. Contacts/AML acquisition and domain-scale studies remain pending.

## Decision

Start with **Internet Topology Zoo**, then add **SocioPatterns high-school
contacts** subject to its usage terms, and **IBM AML-Data**. These exercise
structural failures, observed temporal changes, and transaction multiplicity,
respectively. They are workload sources, not competing connectivity engines.
Keep petgraph as the primary external embedded baseline; retain algorithmic
controls without presenting their popularity as market adoption.

## Adoption: evidence rather than a popularity claim

The objection to relying on obscure competitors is valid. Evaluate software
competitors by users, maintenance, integration and comparable semantics; evaluate
data sources additionally by published methodology and independent reuse.

The Topology Zoo mirror displayed 22 stars and 3 forks on this inspection. That
small mirror is not evidence of commercial adoption. The independently published
[T-Garden dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/21116252) explicitly reuses Topology
Zoo: this supports scientific reuse, without establishing a citation count or a
market ranking. Its old helper tools are described by the maintainer as
unmaintained; use the data format, not those tools.

IBM AML-Data and AMLSim have institutional provenance and accessible artifacts,
but that alone does not establish production adoption. Their published examples
and author papers do not prove independent deployment. SocioPatterns has a
published measurement protocol; independent usage counts were not audited here.
No candidate is labelled an industry standard based on stars or branding.

## What Knotrel can and cannot demonstrate

Knotrel answers undirected, instantaneous connectivity in a simple graph.
Telecom connectivity does not establish routing feasibility, capacity or latency.
Contact connectivity does not establish a time-respecting infection path.
AML connectivity can identify association components, but does not classify fraud,
trace directed money flows, enforce account permissions or prove laundering.
Do not report detection accuracy from a connectivity benchmark.

Node types, amounts, classes and bank identity belong in the importer and
provenance sidecar for now. A class or bank membership must not silently create
edges. Nested graphs and typed traversal require their own query semantics and
ADR; these scenarios will supply concrete requirements before core changes.

## Broader literature and deferred work

[Temporal Graph Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01026) and its
[dataset catalogue](https://tgb-website.pages.dev/docs/dataset_overview/) cover
additional interaction and transaction domains. Its link/node prediction tasks
are different from exact connectivity. Dataset-specific licenses and projection
rules must be checked before selecting an artifact; a software license is not a
data license. Electrical networks remain useful future cases, but require an
explicit distinction between topological reachability and electrical feasibility.

The selected cases are not proof of scale or realism for every application.
Topology archives are historical; contact observations have sampling boundaries;
synthetic AML reflects its generator. Publish those limits beside results.
