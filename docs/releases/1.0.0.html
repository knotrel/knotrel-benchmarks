Reading the tables

Each row has three separate fields: the verdict exactly as the campaign analyzer measured it, its category in the decision rule, and its execution status. No verdict is converted into another.

decisive
Enters the campaign decision rule; a non-PASS verdict needs a recorded maintainer disposition.
descriptive
Reported for context; it does not enter the decision rule.
disclosure
Reported by rule and never converted; it does not block the release.
completed
Measured and analyzed. Estimates are shown; a rule verdict, or a ratio with a zero reference, shows "Not available" and says why.
not run
Not measured (for example, after a product failure stopped its experiment). The reason is shown; there are no numbers.
nonconforming
Its block did not conform to the protocol (for example, a time cap). No verdict and no numbers.
failed
Ran without an analyzable result (a product failure or an invalid observation). The reason is shown; there are no numbers.
